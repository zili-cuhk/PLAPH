## Result files

* `results/simulation/<experiment>/<design>/chunk_*/scenario_001_replicates.csv`: one row per
  replication (seed, acceptance and numerical diagnostics, selected supports, FP/FN counts,
  coefficient estimates and model-based standard errors, IMSE, PL results).
* `.../scenario_001_tuning_path.csv`: every (c_L, kappa) grid fit of every replication (EBIC,
  df, support sizes, acceptance flags, selected point).
* `results/simulation/summary/`: per-design summaries written by `plaph_v2_aggregate.m`, and
  `curves_*.csv` / `function_error_*.csv` written by `plaph_v2_curve_summary.m`.
* `results/simulation/grpsurv_results/<design>/rows_*.csv`: grpsurv results per data set;
  `rows_*_curves.csv`: its centered fitted functions on the evaluation grid.
* `results/tcga/`: fit summaries, gene-level linear coefficients and CNA group norms for each
  TCGA fit (no patient-level data). Absolute output paths have been replaced by `<OUT_ROOT>`.
